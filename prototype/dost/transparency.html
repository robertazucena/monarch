About the Transparency Seal

DOST publishes its mandate and officials, financial reports, budget, programs, procurement and performance in compliance with the General Appropriations Act. A pearl buried inside a tightly shut shell is practically worthless; government information is meant to be shared to maximise its value.

This concept lists the document groups. Each link opens dost.gov.ph, where the files are kept by year, for DOST-OSEC and the Central Office.

Freedom of Information

  1. Read the manual

    The DOST FOI one-page manual explains what you can request and how.

  2. File your request

    Use the FOI request form or the eFOI portal at foi.gov.ph and choose DOST.

  3. Receive the record

    Or a written explanation if an exception applies. DOST publishes its FOI reports.
