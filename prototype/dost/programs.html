S&T Scholarships

Nasa Siyensiya ang Pagasa, Maging DOST Iskolar Ka! The Science Education Institute processes undergraduate and graduate scholarship applications, by direct filing or by mail, and issues clearances to scholar graduates.

  • R.A. 7687 Undergraduate S&T Scholarship
  • DOST-SEI Merit Scholarship
  • Junior Level Science Scholarship-Merit
  • Accelerated S&T Human Resource Development Program (ASTHRDP)
  • Engineering Research and Development for Technology Program (ERDT)
  • Science and Mathematics Teaching Scholarship
  • Bridging Education in Science and Technology for Indigenous Peoples

Regional offices accept applications for the undergraduate S&T scholarship examination. The Philippine Science High School System runs its own National Competitive Examination.

Apply at science-scholarships.phDOST-SEI

SETUP for MSMEs

The Small Enterprise Technology Upgrading Program helps micro, small and medium enterprises upgrade their technology. It runs through the regional offices, alongside their other technology services.

  • Technology transfer and Regional Grants-In-Aid (non-SETUP)
  • Packaging and labelling
  • Technical advisory and consultancy services
  • Cleaner production, energy audit and food safety
  • Manufacturing Productivity Extension Program (MPEX)
  • Technology trainings

Services listed by DOST-NCR; each region offers its own mix.
