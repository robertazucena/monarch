About the Transparency Seal

The Transparency Seal is required of every national government agency under the General Appropriations Act. Clicking it leads to the documents below, kept up to date by the Department.

This concept lists the kinds of documents the Department publishes. Each link opens the official website, where the current files are kept.

Freedom of Information

  1. File your request

    Use the eFOI portal at foi.gov.ph or submit a written request to the Department’s FOI receiving officer.

  2. Wait for a reply

    The Department answers within 15 working days, extendable in some cases.

  3. Receive the information

    Or a written explanation if an exception under the FOI manual applies.
