The Foreign Service Officers’ Examination

Passing the exam is the way into the career foreign service as a Foreign Service Officer.

  1. Qualifying test

    A written test of general knowledge, reasoning and language skills.

  2. Written examination

    Essays on international relations, economics and Philippine affairs.

  3. Psychological evaluation

    Tests and an interview to assess fitness for service abroad.

  4. Panel interview

    A conversation with senior officers about your motivation and judgement.

  5. Oral examination

    The final stage before a board of senior officials.

Eligibility rules, dates and requirements are published in each year’s examination announcement. Read the current one before you apply.

How to apply

  1. Find an opening

    Vacancies are posted on the Civil Service Commission Job Portal and the DFA website.

  2. Prepare your documents

    Usually a letter of intent, personal data sheet, transcript and certificate of eligibility.

  3. Submit before the deadline

    Follow the instructions in the posting. Incomplete applications are not considered.
