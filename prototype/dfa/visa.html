Visa-free entry

Nationals of more than 150 countries may enter the Philippines without a visa for up to 30 days for tourism or business. Some nationalities are allowed longer stays under bilateral agreements.

  • A passport valid for at least six months beyond your planned stay
  • A confirmed return or onward ticket
  • eTravel registration completed before arrival

Rules differ by nationality and change from time to time. Check with the Philippine Embassy or Consulate General that covers your country before you book travel.

Apply online with the e-Visa

  1. Check you are eligible

    The e-Visa covers applicants in selected countries. The portal tells you if yours is one.

  2. Create an account at visa.gov.ph

    Fill in the form and upload your passport and supporting documents.

  3. Pay the visa fee online

    Fees depend on the visa type and the number of entries.

  4. Receive your e-Visa by email

    Print it or keep it on your phone, and show it to the airline and immigration.

On arrival

Register at etravel.gov.ph within 72 hours before departure. It is free. Keep the QR code ready for the airline and the immigration officer.

Immigration officers may ask for your return ticket, hotel booking and proof that you can support yourself during your stay.
