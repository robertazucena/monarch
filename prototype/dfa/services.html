    Proceed to official appointment portal

    Before your appointment

    1. Book online, for free

      Use only passport.gov.ph or apostille.gov.ph. Nobody is authorised to sell slots.

    2. Prepare originals and photocopies

      Bring every document in original with one photocopy, on A4 paper.

    3. Arrive 30 minutes early

      Late applicants may be asked to rebook. Courtesy Lane applicants can walk in.

    4. Pay at the office or online

      Keep your official receipt. You need it to claim your passport or documents.
