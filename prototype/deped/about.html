Our mandate

DepEd formulates, implements and coordinates the policies, plans, programs and projects of formal and non-formal basic education. It supervises every public and private elementary and secondary school, and the Alternative Learning System.

Its current mandate comes from the Governance of Basic Education Act of 2001 (Republic Act 9155), which renamed the Department of Education, Culture and Sports and set out how the Central Office, regions, divisions and schools share the work.

Vision and mission

Vision

We dream of Filipinos who passionately love their country and whose values and competencies enable them to realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to building the nation.

As a learner-centered public institution, the Department of Education continuously improves itself to better serve its stakeholders.

Mission: to protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education where:

Learners

learn in a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe and motivating environment.

Teachers

facilitate learning and constantly nurture every learner.

Administrators and staff

as stewards of the institution, ensure an enabling and supportive environment for effective learning to happen.

Family, community and stakeholders

are actively engaged and share responsibility for developing life-long learners.

History

  1. 1863
    A school in every town

    The Educational Decree of 1863 creates the Superior Commission of Primary Instruction and calls for a primary school in each town.

  2. 1898
    Schools reopen

    Schools closed during the Revolution reopen on 29 August 1898.

  3. 1901
    A public school system

    Act No. 74 of the Philippine Commission sets up a centralised public school system; 600 teachers arrive from the United States.

  4. 1947
    The Department of Education

    Executive Order No. 94 renames the Department of Instruction the Department of Education.

  5. 1987
    DECS

    Executive Order No. 117 makes it the Department of Education, Culture and Sports.

  6. 1994
    A focus on basic education

    CHED and TESDA are created for higher and technical education, leaving DECS to focus on basic education.

  7. 2001
    DepEd

    The Governance of Basic Education Act (RA 9155) renames DECS the Department of Education.

  8. 2013
    K to 12

    The Enhanced Basic Education Act (RA 10533) adds Kindergarten and Senior High School to make basic education 13 years.

  9. 2026
    Strengthened Senior High School

    The strengthened SHS program moves to full implementation in School Year 2026–2027.

Organisation

DepEd works at four levels: the Central Office in Pasig City, regional offices, schools division offices and the schools themselves.

  • Office of the SecretaryLed by Secretary Sonny Angara, with undersecretaries for strands such as Learning Systems, Governance and Operations, Strategic Management, Finance, Administration, and ICT.
  • Curriculum and learning bureausCurriculum Development, Learning Delivery, Education Assessment and Learning Resources.
  • Learner supportThe Bureau of Learner Support Services and the Learner Rights and Protection Office.
  • Bureau of Alternative EducationRuns the Alternative Learning System for out-of-school youth and adults.
  • National Educators Academy of the PhilippinesProfessional development for teachers and school leaders.
  • Regional and division officesSixteen regional offices oversee schools division offices, which supervise the schools in each province or city.
