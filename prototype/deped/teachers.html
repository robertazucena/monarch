The teacher laptop program

231,725Teacher laptops awarded
169,008MacBooks
62,717Windows laptops
5 yrsWarranty on every unit

Distribution began in Dagupan City on 9 November 2026. Teachers receiving a laptop for the first time get priority. Units can be disabled remotely if they are sold, and DepEd and NEAP run GuroXL training toward Apple Teacher certification. Suppliers have until March 2027 to deliver.
