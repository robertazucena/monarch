Vacancies are posted on deped.gov.ph by organisational strand, each with its bureau or service, item number, position and closing date.

  • Education Program Specialist IICurriculum and learning bureaus
  • Statistician IIPlanning and research

  • Engineer IIEducation facilities
  • Legal Assistant IILegal and legislative affairs

These are examples from the current list of 28 positions, all closing on 30 November 2026. Open the official list for the full set and each job’s requirements.

Teaching positions

Teacher I positions in public schools are filled by each Schools Division Office. Watch your division’s postings and submit your application there.

How to apply

  1. Find an opening

    Central Office jobs are posted on deped.gov.ph; teaching posts at your Schools Division Office.

  2. Prepare your documents

    Usually a letter of application, a Personal Data Sheet, your transcript and proof of eligibility.

  3. Submit before the closing date

    Follow the instructions in the posting. Late or incomplete applications are not considered.
