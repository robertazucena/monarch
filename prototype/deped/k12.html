The four stages

  1. Kindergarten1 year

    The first step of compulsory basic education.

  2. ElementaryGrades 1 to 6

    Literacy, numeracy and the foundations of every subject.

  3. Junior High SchoolGrades 7 to 10

    Core subjects, with exploratory technology and livelihood courses.

  4. Senior High SchoolGrades 11 and 12

    Academic or Tech-Pro track, preparing for college, work or business.

The MATATAG curriculum

The revised Kindergarten to Grade 10 curriculum, known as MATATAG, trims crowded subjects so learners can master foundational skills such as reading and mathematics first. It was rolled out in phases, grade by grade.

Teachers are trained for it through the National Educators Academy of the Philippines.

Curriculum guides for each grade and subject are published on deped.gov.ph under the Revised K to 10 Curriculum.

Curriculum guides

Strengthened Senior High School

Grade 11 and 12 learners choose a track and electives by interest, for college, employment, technical skills and National Certifications, or entrepreneurship.

Academic track

Elective clusters in Arts, Social Science and Humanities; Business and Entrepreneurship; STEM; Sports, Health and Wellness; and Field Experience.

Tech-Pro track

Ten technical-professional clusters, such as Agri-Fishery Business and Food Innovation, Hospitality and Tourism, ICT Support and Computer Programming, and Maritime.

Core subjects for every learner

  • Effective Communication
  • Mabisang Komunikasyon
  • General Mathematics
  • General Science
  • Life and Career Skills
  • Pag-aaral ng Kasaysayan at Lipunang Pilipino

Work immersion and field experience

The Field Experience cluster includes Research, Design and Innovation, in-campus field exposure for Sports, and Arts Apprenticeship. DepEd Order 023, s. 2026 sets the rules for work immersion, off-campus field exposure and arts apprenticeship, to make them safer and more meaningful for learners.

Read DO 023, s. 2026

Roll-out and school calendar

  1. School Year 2025–2026

    Grade 11 pilot in selected schools (DM 48, s. 2025).

  2. School Year 2026–2027

    Grade 12 pilot (DM 36, s. 2026) and full implementation (DM 12, s. 2026).

Public schools running the strengthened program follow a three-term calendar. Private schools may keep the semestral calendar.
