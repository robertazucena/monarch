About the Transparency Seal

The Transparency Seal leads to the documents below, kept current by the Department: its mandate and officials, financial and physical reports, budget, procurement and performance.

This concept lists the document groups. Each link opens deped.gov.ph, where the current files are kept.

Freedom of Information

  1. File your request

    Use the eFOI portal at foi.gov.ph and choose the Department of Education.

  2. Wait for a reply

    Agencies answer within 15 working days, which can be extended in some cases.

  3. Receive the record

    Or a written explanation if an exception applies.
