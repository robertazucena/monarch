Adopt-a-School and Brigada Eskwela

Companies, organisations and individuals can support public schools with resources, repairs, equipment and volunteer work. Partners may apply for tax incentives for their contributions, and DepEd provides memorandum of agreement and donation templates.

Learner rights and protection

The Learner Rights and Protection Office carries DepEd’s child protection policies, learning materials and courses on learner safety, and the Learners TeleSafe Contact Center helpline.

If a learner is in danger, contact the school head or call the Public Assistance and Action Center at (02) 8638-8641.

DepEd Computerization Program

The program equips schools and teachers with devices. In November 2026 it began handing out more than 231,000 laptops to public school teachers, starting in Dagupan City.
